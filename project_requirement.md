SkillSwap: Trade Skills Instead of Money

Concept: Students swap skills with each other (guitar lessons for math tutoring) using time as currency instead of cash.

Functionality:
Create a profile listing what you can teach and what you want to learn.
Search for people by skill.
Request a session with someone; when it's done, time credit moves from one person to the other.
See your current time credit balance.

Leave a short review after a session.
Filter search results by availability or rating.

Direct messaging so both sides can agree on a time and place before confirming.
A "skills wishlist" that suggests matching teachers automatically.
Badges for milestones (first trade, 10 trades completed, top-rated teacher).
A public profile page others can browse, showing skills, ratings, and past trades.
An admin panel to handle disputes when two people disagree about whether a trade happened.
A leaderboard of the most active or highest-rated members each month.

Everyone has a reliability score that quietly drops if they go quiet or ghost a trade.
Fall too low, and you're temporarily blocked from requesting new trades until you do something to earn trust back.
